## Scope

Home page (single-page marketing site) plus contact API, privacy page, 404 and a project-detail template. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: founders, business owners and enterprise buyers choosing an AI/software engineering partner. Action: start a project (contact form). Proof available: none (no clients, metrics or case studies), so the page proves depth through specifics: real artefacts per service, an architecture-style stack, process deliverables. Constraints: owner-pinned palette, type and hero direction; no fabricated proof.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Tredrox builds systems in layers, so the page is drawn like an engineered stack, not a brochure. The brand object is a glass layer-stack with a lit core; sections show the work as diagrams, flows and specs. Refuses the category default: centred headline and two buttons over a gradient, then rows of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: #05080D ground under a faint blueprint hairline grid; cobalt light (#2F6BFF to #5B8CFF) is the only saturated colour; mint (#5EF2C0) only marks live or running state; off-white #EDF2F7 type, muted #8B95A7. Geist 500 display tracked -0.04em, Geist Mono only for code and data. Glass lives on the hero object and the one product frame; elsewhere 1px low-opacity borders and a 14px radius family.

STORY: in one screen the visitor learns Tredrox builds AI products and software for businesses; scrolling shows how (service modules with their artefacts, the exploded product stack, the request path through the stack, the process with deliverables), believes it because nothing is inflated, and starts a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: nav on top; no edge hairlines (removed on owner feedback), and the blueprint grid lives inside the object's light cone, bent by the glass (revised after the first finish review: grid lines crossing the h1 hurt legibility). H1 in three lines over columns 1-8 at up to 6rem; the WebGL glass stack sits right of centre on a canvas spanning the right ~64%, never overlapping the headline (owner feedback: the overlap read as a collision). Supporting line, Start a Project (cobalt, primary) and See What We Build (secondary; the portfolio ships empty, so it points at the services) bottom-left; two small live system chips beside the object; the technology marquee is the hero's bottom band.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — owner: "Create an original 3D-inspired abstract technology composition... a luminous geometric structure, connected intelligent nodes, a digital core, or layered glass objects with electric blue lighting" and "Background: Deep charcoal / near-black (#05080D or similar). Primary accent: Electric blue / cobalt blue. Secondary accent: Subtle mint or neon green." Signature interaction (revised on owner feedback): the hero object is an AI processor on a circuit board that computes and moves data, leans to the pointer, works harder on hover and pulses on click; Solutions keeps the brand's stacked plates as a scroll-driven exploded product stack whose layers name what Tredrox builds.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
