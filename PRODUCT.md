## Platform

web

## Stack

Pinned by the owner's brief: Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React, Motion for React. Three.js is allowed for the hero visual. Contact form posts to a Next.js API route that sends email through an environment-configured provider (no hardcoded credentials).

## Users

<!-- Inferred from the owner's brief; the owner gave a free hand instead of an interview (2026-11-18). -->
- Startup founders and entrepreneurs with a product idea who need an engineering partner to design, build and ship it.
- Owners and operators of established businesses who want AI, automation or integrations to remove manual work.
- Technical and non-technical decision makers at larger companies ("international enterprise clients") evaluating a vendor for an AI or software build.

Their job on the site: decide within a minute whether Tredrox is a serious engineering partner, find whether it covers their kind of project, and start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Tredrox Systems is a technology company that builds intelligent software, AI-powered applications and business automation for clients. The website exists to win qualified project enquiries. Success is a visitor submitting the contact form with a real project description.

## Positioning

An engineering-focused technology partner, not a generic web development agency: Tredrox designs, builds and deploys intelligent digital products end to end, combining software engineering (SaaS, APIs, backends, databases) with practical AI (agents, LLM integrations, assistants, MCP servers, automation).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Tredrox Systems ("Tredrox" in short). No logo asset exists; a typographic wordmark is to be created.
- Owner-pinned visual direction: deep near-black ground (#05080D or similar), electric/cobalt blue as primary accent, subtle mint as secondary, off-white and muted grey text, thin low-opacity borders, controlled glow and glass (not overused), premium modern sans (Geist, Inter or Manrope), oversized editorial headings, abstract 3D technology visual in the hero.
- Owner-supplied headings for hero, about, services, projects, why, process and contact are client copy and stay unless the owner changes them.
- References are inspiration only: synthcoder.com, masterofcode.com, hatchworks.com, phaselink.ai and five Dribbble-style screenshots. Never copy layout, text, branding or assets.
- Banned by the owner: purple-heavy gradients, random glowing circles, robot illustrations, generic AI stock imagery, Bootstrap-like layouts, dashboard look, identical repeated cards, walls of text, Lorem Ipsum.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. There are no case studies, client names, testimonials, metrics, awards or partner logos. Future work must not fabricate any of them. The portfolio is data-driven (`src/content/projects.ts`) and ships empty until the owner adds real projects.

## Product Principles

1. Prove engineering depth through specifics (architectures, workflows, stacks, deliverables), never through claims or numbers we cannot back.
2. Business problem first, technology second: every capability is described by the work it removes or the outcome it enables.
3. Honest by default: empty states over invented proof; anything illustrative is plainly illustrative.
4. Every enquiry path works: the primary action (start a project) is reachable from every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the working standard: contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion support, pausable moving content.
